Study Description

A randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled Phase III clinical trial evaluating the efficacy and safety of CardioGuard (CG-101) in patients with moderate-to-severe heart failure. The study aims to demonstrate a statistically significant reduction in major adverse cardiovascular events (MACE) compared to standard of care.
